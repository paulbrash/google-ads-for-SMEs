The Problem

Why Most Small Business Google Ads Don't Deliver

Google Ads is a powerful tool — but without the right setup and ongoing management, it quietly drains budget while delivering little in return.

Set Up Once, Never Touched

Many accounts are built quickly and then left to run. Without regular optimisation, performance degrades and budget leaks into searches that will never convert.

Targeting the Wrong People

Broad keyword settings and poor audience targeting means paying for clicks from people who were never going to become customers — at full cost per click.

Ads That Don't Convert

Writing ads that speak to small business customers is a craft. Generic ad copy that doesn't match what searchers need drives clicks but not enquiries.

No Visibility Into Performance

Without clear data on what's performing, it's impossible to know which campaigns are driving real business outcomes — and which are just spending money with nothing to show for it.

Burned by Bad Advice

Google's own recommendations are designed to increase spend, not efficiency. Following them without scrutiny often makes performance worse, not better.

No Time to Learn It Yourself

Google Ads is complex and changes constantly. Running a business leaves little time to master it — and half-measures often do more harm than good.

There's a better way. Google Ads works extremely well for small businesses when set up correctly and managed consistently. The difference is expert attention — the kind that most small business owners don't have time for, but we do.

Is This Right For You?

A Good Fit — and When It Isn't

We'd rather be upfront about who this service works well for than take on a client where we can't genuinely add value.

Good fit
  • You have a product or service with clear demand that people search for
  • Your monthly Google Ads budget is $500 or more
  • You're willing to invest in management alongside your ad spend
  • You want results and can measure them (leads, sales, calls)
  • You've tried Google Ads before and feel like it should be working better
  • You're starting fresh and want to get it right first time
Not the right fit
  • Your budget is under $500/month — there's typically not enough to work with effectively
  • You're looking for guaranteed results — no one can honestly promise those
  • You need leads tomorrow — Google Ads takes weeks to optimise properly
  • You want to retain full control of day-to-day changes yourself
  • Your product or service has no meaningful search demand
